FAQ

Common questions

How long does setup take?

Most companies go live within one to two weeks. We import your employee data, leave rules and salary structures for you.

Can employees use it on their phones?

Yes. Employees can clock in, apply for leave and download payslips from any phone browser or the mobile app.

Does payroll handle PF, ESI and PT?

Yes. Statutory deductions are calculated automatically, and monthly compliance reports are ready to download.

Can we connect our biometric machines?

Yes. Biometric attendance devices can be synced on the Enterprise plan. Tell us your device model during the demo.

Is our data safe?

Access is role-based, logins are encrypted, and data is backed up regularly. Only people you authorise can see salary information.
